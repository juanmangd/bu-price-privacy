Bu Price privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version

Your prices, items and stores are saved only on this phone. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What leaves your phone

When you scan a barcode we don’t know yet, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the item’s name. Nothing about you is sent with it.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes. No pictures or videos are taken or saved.

Location

If you choose “I’m at this store now”, the store’s location is saved on this phone so Bu Price can pick that store next time. It is never sent anywhere.

Your choices

You can save a copy of your data, or delete all of it, any time in Settings. Deleting the app also deletes your data.

Questions

Email juanman.gd@gmail.com and we’ll be happy to help.

Open Food Facts

Barcode lookups are answered by Open Food Facts, a free database built by volunteers. Their own privacy policy applies to the lookups they receive.
